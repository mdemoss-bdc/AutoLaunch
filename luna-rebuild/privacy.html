Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This policy covers the AutoLaunch web application and the AutoLaunch Vehicle Assistant Chrome extension (together, "AutoLaunch"). AutoLaunch is a listing syndication tool for car dealerships and independent sales representatives: it takes a dealership's own vehicle inventory and posts those vehicles to sales channels — including Facebook Marketplace, through the extension — at the user's direction.

What data AutoLaunch handles

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in credentials, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We use your email to identify your account.
  • Dealership and inventory data: your dealership name, your inventory feed URLs, and the vehicle data they produce — year, make, model, trim, VIN, stock number, price, mileage, colors, and listing photos.
  • Posting settings and queue jobs: your posting preferences (such as daily posting limits, automation choices, and appearance settings), your customer wishlist entries, and the queue of posting/removal jobs you create, including each job's status and any failure reason.
  • Facebook interaction, locally in your browser: the extension works inside your own signed-in Facebook session in Chrome. It fills in the Marketplace vehicle form, and deletes listings you ask it to remove, using the data from your queue job. Your Facebook password is never seen, collected, or stored by AutoLaunch — Facebook's own sign-in stays between you and Facebook.
  • Your AutoLaunch session token, in the extension: when you are signed in to AutoLaunch, the app hands your session token to the extension so the extension can read and update your own queue. The token is stored only in the extension's local storage on your own computer, is used only as the sign-in credential for your AutoLaunch backend requests, and is never logged or sent anywhere else.

How the data is used

Data is used only to operate AutoLaunch for you: syncing your inventory, building and carrying out your posting queue, posting and removing your listings on the channels you enable, showing you job status and failure reasons, and keeping your account secure. That is the only purpose.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data, and we do not rent or share it with third parties for their own marketing or any other purpose of theirs.
  • We run no third-party analytics or advertising trackers in the app or the extension.
  • The extension does not collect your browsing history and does not read pages outside the AutoLaunch app, Facebook Marketplace, and the vehicle photo locations contained in your own queue jobs.

Where data lives and how it is protected

  • Account, inventory, settings, and queue data are stored in AutoLaunch's backend database (Supabase), where access is locked per user: each account can only see and modify its own rows.
  • Platform keys and service secrets are Matthew's (the platform operator's), are kept server-side only, and are never shipped to the app, the extension, or end users. End users never handle API keys.
  • Photos for a listing are fetched by the extension from the dealership photo source in your queue job and attached to your listing on Facebook; they are not copied to any AutoLaunch analytics or advertising system.

Your choices

  • You control posting: the extension's Confirm mode is the default, and nothing is published until you click Publish (or deliberately turn on Full Auto in Settings).
  • You can remove a listing at any time from AutoLaunch, sign out (which ends the session the extension relies on), uninstall the extension, and ask us to delete your account data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the updated page will be posted here with a new "last updated" date before the change takes effect for new data.
