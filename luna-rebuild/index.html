Facebook Marketplace

Marketplace posting runs through the AutoLaunch Chrome extension, inside your own signed-in Facebook session — there is no token or key to paste here, by design. Cars you queue are picked up by the extension and posted with your daily safety limit.

Facebook Page

Auto-post new arrivals

ALL new arrivals from your website get queued automatically — no review before they post. Save a filter with Auto-post on to limit which ones.

Logos and "photo coming soon" pictures don't count. A car still waiting for photos posts by itself once they're on your website.

Auto delete & repost

Automatically deletes and reposts listings that have been up too long — fresh listings rank higher in Marketplace search.

Runs from your browser — it must be on with Chrome open and the extension signed in. Posts respect your daily limit.

Cars that sell or leave your website are never reposted.

Automatic photo edits

Set it once — every vehicle you post gets these edits automatically. Included in your plan.

Pick a background on the right — your choice is saved and used as the posting background. The preview cuts the vehicle out and places it on the background right here on your device (first preview downloads the AI model once).
