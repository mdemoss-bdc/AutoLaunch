Auto-post new arrivals

ALL new arrivals from your website get queued automatically — no review before they post. Save a filter with Auto-post on to limit which ones.

Logos and "photo coming soon" pictures don't count. A car still waiting for photos posts by itself once they're on your website.

Auto delete & repost

Automatically deletes and reposts listings that have been up too long — fresh listings rank higher in Marketplace search.

Runs from your browser — it must be on with Chrome open and the extension signed in. Posts respect your daily limit.

Cars that sell or leave your website are never reposted.
