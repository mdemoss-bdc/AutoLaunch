What AutoLaunch does

A lightweight, high-speed marketing and listing syndication suite. It is deliberately not a full CRM — no desking, no credit tools, no phone system. It does one job: get your cars in front of more buyers, faster, and keep the listings managed for you.

🚗 Automatic Marketplace posting

Your synced inventory posts to Facebook Marketplace through the AutoLaunch browser helper, using your own signed-in Facebook account. Set your daily pace and let it run while you sell.

🔄 Delete & repost, handled

Sold a car? Its listing comes down automatically. Want fresh eyes on a stale unit? Auto delete & repost refreshes it on your schedule.

💲 Price updates that follow your site

When a price changes on your dealership website, the Marketplace listing is updated to match — your advertised final price, never a stale number.

✨ Rich listing descriptions

Listings are written from each car's real data — features, factory packages, and specs pulled from the vehicle's own page. No invented equipment, no guesswork.

👥 Prospects + Luca, your drafting assistant

A simple prospect tracker (New → Talking → Visit set → Came in → Sold) with Luca, an assistant that drafts follow-up replies in your voice. Luca never sends anything — you review and send every message yourself.

🎯 Customer Wishlist

Record what a customer wants — make, model, year, price, miles — and AutoLaunch watches your inventory and flags the match the moment it lands.

🔁 Trade appraisal link

Every rep gets a personal trade-in link. Buyers answer a short condition survey and send photos; the full appraisal packet lands in your dashboard.

📣 More channels

Facebook Page posting is built in, and TikTok and Instagram posting are rolling out on the Automate plan — the same queue, the same one-time entry.

💬 Ask Luna support

Help lives inside the app. Ask Luna answers questions about your account and your listings, any time — no ticket queue, no waiting on hold.

How it works

Three steps between your lot and your buyers.

1

Connect your inventory

Point AutoLaunch at your dealership's inventory feed. Your cars sync in automatically and stay current — new arrivals in, sold units out.

2

Install the helper

Add the free AutoLaunch Vehicle Assistant extension to Chrome and sign in. It does the posting in your own browser session, on your own Facebook account.

3

Cars post themselves

Pick your pace and preferences once. AutoLaunch queues, posts, updates prices, and pulls sold cars down — while you work your deals.

Questions

Who is AutoLaunch for?

Franchise and independent dealerships, and the sales reps who work their own social selling. If you post cars to Facebook Marketplace by hand today, AutoLaunch is built for you.

Do I post from my own Facebook account?

Yes. Marketplace vehicle listings are posted through your own signed-in Facebook session in Chrome, using the AutoLaunch helper extension. You stay in control of your account at all times.

Does AutoLaunch replace my CRM?

No — on purpose. AutoLaunch handles inventory, listings, and a light prospect tracker. Your CRM keeps desking, credit, and phones. A lead handoff export keeps the two connected.

How does the free trial work?

Sign up and you get 4 days free, no card up front: days 1–2 run exactly like Self Post, days 3–4 run exactly like Automate. When the trial ends, pick a plan to keep going.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from Settings, through the billing portal. Your access runs to the end of the period you've paid for.
